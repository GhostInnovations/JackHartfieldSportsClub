Membership

Anyone who has not paid their subscriptions for this year or any other years can now pay them to the Secretary Mick Mead on Wednesday nights, Friday nights, or Saturday nights between 8.00pm & 9.00pm. This must only be paid to Mick and must be paid before they can use the club. We are also taking new members but this must only be paid to the Secretary Mick Mead and will only be taken on Wednesday nights, Friday nights, and Saturday nights between 8.00pm & 9.00pm.

A joining fee of £20 together with a £5 yearly subscription (£25 total), is required for the first year. Thereafter the annual subscription is £5 and is normally due on 1st February of each year.

Subscriptions can be paid within the month of February but after this period, if membership has not been renewed then entry to Jack Hatfield Sports Club facilities could be refused. Subscriptions can be paid for 2023 from :- 5th February to 3rd March 2023

Between 7pm – 8pm

Any members renewing their membership after 3rd March 2023 time will be charged an additional £1.00
