Room Hire

> The function room seats 110 people and is available for £70 to non-members, £100 for commercial enterprises , and it is free to members. The function room is available from 10am to 11pm, subject to availability. We have held a large variety of events in the past: Weddings, funerals, birthdays, engagements, kids party’s, clairvoyants, games nights, race nights, rokas, community meetings, The Womens Institute, book launches…

Unfortunately, the function room is unavailable on Wednesday, Fridays and Saturday evening due to club activities.

We do not hold 18th or 21st birthdays
